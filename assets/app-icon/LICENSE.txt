Pluma app icon (vectorcraft.svg, vectorcraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in this
directory, and the web favicon where the project has one).

The icon is a portrait of René Descartes cut out, converted to black and white and placed over the
app colour. It belongs to the Nodalia icon family: one historical figure per app.

Source portrait: "Portret van René Descartes René Descartes (titel op object), RP-P-1910-4588.jpg", from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portret_van_René_Descartes_René_Descartes_(titel_op_object),_RP-P-1910-4588.jpg).
